Step 1

Connect your free key

Songsmith runs on Hugging Face's free Inference API. Get a personal key at huggingface.co — it costs nothing, and the key stays only in this browser, never sent anywhere but Hugging Face.

How do I get a free token?
  1. Go to huggingface.co/join and make a free account.
  2. Visit huggingface.co/settings/tokens.
  3. Click New token, give it any name, choose the Read role, then create it.
  4. Copy the token (starts with hf_) and paste it above.

That's it — no card required. Free accounts get a generous number of monthly requests, plenty for making a few tracks.

Tips for better results

The first generation is slow — is that normal?

Yes. Free models "sleep" when unused, so the first request wakes the model up and can take 20–60 seconds, sometimes longer for the larger models. Songsmith will keep checking automatically — just leave the tab open.

I got an error about rate limits

Free Hugging Face accounts have a monthly request budget. If you hit it, wait a bit and try again, or create a second free account for a fresh quota.

Can I use this for more than holiday songs?

Absolutely — try birthday themes, lullabies, road-trip playlists, or backing tracks for home videos. The prompt is the whole instrument.
